Privacy Policy

PLG Apps · Applies to all apps published by PLG Apps on the Atlassian Marketplace · Last updated 17 August 2026

Summary

Our apps run entirely on Atlassian-hosted infrastructure. We operate no servers of our own, we transmit no data to third parties, and we hold no copy of your content. There is nothing for us to sell, share, or lose.

What our apps process

Rich Text Notes — Dashboard Gadget for Jira processes only what a user deliberately puts into a note:

  • The formatted text of the note, including any links it contains.
  • Images that a user chooses to upload to a note.

The app does not read your Jira issues, projects, users, or any other product data. It does not collect account identifiers, email addresses, IP addresses, or usage analytics.

Where that content is stored

Note content is stored in the Atlassian Forge key-value store, and uploaded images in the Atlassian Forge Object Store. Both are provided and operated by Atlassian, within your own Atlassian cloud environment. The app declares no external domains at all, which you can verify yourself on its permissions screen in the Atlassian Marketplace.

Consequently we have no access to your content, no database containing it, and no ability to export it.

Sub-processors

None. Atlassian is the sole processor of any data our apps store, under your existing agreement with Atlassian.

Retention and deletion

  • Note content is overwritten when a user edits the note.
  • When an image stops being referenced by any note, the app deletes it from the Object Store. Uploaded images also carry an expiry, so anything left unreferenced by an unexpected removal is discarded automatically.
  • Storage belongs to the app installation on your site. Removing the app removes its storage, in accordance with Atlassian's handling of Forge app data.

Personal data

We do not copy or store personal data in systems of our own, because we have none. Note text is authored by your users and may contain whatever they choose to write; it is stored only inside your Atlassian environment and is never transmitted elsewhere.

Security

Content shown in a note is sanitised before it is displayed, so a shared dashboard cannot be used to run scripts in another user's browser. Links inside notes open in a new tab and are prevented from accessing the originating page.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what our apps do with your content, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
