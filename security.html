Security Policy

PLG Apps · Applies to all apps published by PLG Apps Studio on the Atlassian Marketplace · Last updated 17 August 2026

Reporting a vulnerability

Email plgapps.support@gmail.com with the subject line SECURITY. Please include the app name, the affected Atlassian product, and enough detail to reproduce the issue.

We acknowledge security reports within two business days and prioritise them over other work. We will tell you what we found and when a fix ships.

Please give us a reasonable opportunity to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly. We do not operate a bug bounty programme and cannot offer payment, but we will credit reporters who ask to be credited.

What the attack surface actually is

Our apps run entirely on Atlassian-hosted infrastructure. We operate no servers, databases, load balancers, or administrative consoles of our own, and our apps declare no external domains. There is no infrastructure of ours to compromise, and no copy of customer data outside Atlassian cloud.

In practice this means the meaningful surface is the app's own code: how it handles content a user enters, and how it uses the Atlassian platform APIs.

In scope

  • Cross-site scripting or content injection through note content.
  • Any path that lets one user read or modify content they should not have access to.
  • Incorrect handling of Atlassian platform permissions or scopes.
  • Vulnerable third-party dependencies shipped in the app bundle.

Out of scope

  • Vulnerabilities in Atlassian's own products or platform — please report those to Atlassian.
  • Findings that require an attacker to already hold administrative access to the customer's Atlassian site.
  • Automated scanner output with no demonstrated impact.
  • Denial of service against Atlassian infrastructure.

How we handle content

Content displayed by our apps is sanitised before rendering, using an allow-list of tags, attributes and URL schemes, so a shared dashboard cannot be used to execute scripts in another user's browser. Links are forced to open in a new tab without access to the originating page.

Dependencies and updates

Third-party dependencies are pinned and reviewed when the app is updated. Fixes are deployed through Atlassian Forge, which updates every installation without customers needing to take action.

What we do not claim

We are an independent developer. We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification, we do not run third-party penetration tests, and we do not operate a bug bounty. We state this plainly because the strength of this approach is that there is no infrastructure of ours to audit — not that audits have been performed.
